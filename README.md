

A simple patch that allow custom color on a sling star.



For custom color :



Obj\_Arg6 to 1 = Bronze



Obj\_Arg6 to 2 = Green



Obj\_Arg6 to 3 = Red



Obj\_Arg6 to 4 = Blue



Obj\_Arg6 to 5 = Silver



Obj\_Arg6 to 6 = White



Obj\_Arg6 to 7 = Rainbow



Obj\_Arg6 to 8 = Purple



Obj\_Arg6 to 9 = Pink
